                      Targeting Visor

                      Increases the wearers chances to hit targets at a range of 10 or further by 20%.

                      Targeting Visor is a Helmet in Rogue Trader. Accessories are items that provide unique defensive stats, effects, bonuses, skill check bonuses, and more when they are equipped. Targeting Visor is an armor piece that  increases the wearer's chances to hit targets at a range of 10 or further by 20%

                       

                      Rogue Trader Targeting Visor Information

                      Targeting Visor provides the following effect:

                      • Increases the wearers chances to hit targets at a range of 10 or further by 20%.

                      To equip Targeting Visor players need to meet the following prerequisites:

                      • Aeldari Equipment

                       

                      How to Obtain Targeting Visor in Rogue Trader

                      Targeting Visor can be found at:

                      • Can be purchased from Opticon-22
