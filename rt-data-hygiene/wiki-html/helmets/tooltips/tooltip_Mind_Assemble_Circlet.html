Mind Assemble Circlet

Whenever the wearer uses a psychic power, they can  trigger one of the following:

  • 50% chance to reduce the AP cost and decrease veil degradation by -2
  • 25% chance to perform the psychic power as usual
  • 25% chance to increase veil degradation by +3 and reset the cooldown of their attack

If veil degradation is increased to 15 or beyond, this effect triggers perils of the warp even when using minor psychic powers.
