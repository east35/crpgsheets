                      Helmets | Rogue Trader Wiki


                      Helmets are one of the accessories that can be equipped by players. Each accessory contain its unique defensive Stats, effects, bonuses, skill checks, and more when they are equipped. Accessories are usually obtained as rewards from completing Quests, purchased from Merchants, looted from various locations, crafted, or are dropped by Enemies and Bosses. This page will indicate a list of all the Helmets in Warhammer 40k: Rogue Trader which will include its description and values. You can find detailed information on its location and properties on each individual page linked below.

                      Pirate's Eyepatch

                      This scarf grants its wearer a +20 bonus to Coercion and applies a -5 penalty to Awareness.

                      		  

                      Poet's Grace

                      The wearer gains +25 Commerce and +25 Persuasion.

                      		  

                      Sniper's Bravado

                      Whenever the wearer's single-shot ranged attack deals damage equal to or more than 25% of the target's maximum wounds, this attack randomly inflicts blinded, staggered, or slowed on that target for 1 round.

                      		  

                      Spinner's Mask

                      This mask of a messenger of death is as worn as a burial shroud. The gaze of its impassive bony visage carries an unyielding promise: every life must end.

                      If the wearer has 10 or more stacks of bloodblessed, they gain +1 resolve.

                      		  

                      Tamer of the Empyrean

                      If psychic phenomena or perils of warp are triggered, the wearer gains +1 psy rating during their next turn.

                      		  

                      Visor of Inevitability

                      Whenever the wearer inflicts a damage-over-time effect, they gain +1 stack of the inevitability effect.

                      As soon as the wearer gains 10 stacks of inevitability, they deal XX direct damage to all enemies suffering from any damage-over-time effects, and all the stacks of inevitability are removed.

                      		  

                      Zero-Sum Lens

                      The wearer has a (resolve)% chance to reduce any incoming damage to 0.
